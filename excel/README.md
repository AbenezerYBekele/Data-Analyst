## Vehicle Management
 
Excel workbook analyzing a fleet of 71 institutional vehicles across 7 departments using AutoFilter, Subtotals, sorting, and PivotTables.
 
## Dataset Fields
 
`ID #` · `YEAR` · `MAKE` · `TYPE` · `ODOMETER` · `DEPARTMENT ASSIGNED` · `PURCHASE PRICE` · `MAINTENANCE COST-ANNUAL`
 
- **Makes:** Chevrolet, Ford, Dodge, GMC
- **Types:** Sedan, Pickup, Truck, Van
- **Departments:** Athletics, Dining Services, Housing & Residential Life, Police & Security, Printing Services, Property, Telecomm
